# Creative Director: Animation Vision & Leadership

> Source: [dylantarre/animation-principles — skills/03-by-role-persona/creative-director/SKILL.md](https://github.com/dylantarre/animation-principles/blob/main/skills/03-by-role-persona/creative-director/SKILL.md) (MIT License)

Key direction questions for every production:
1. **Squash & Stretch**: Where on the rigid-to-rubbery spectrum does this world live?
2. **Anticipation**: Do we let moments breathe, or punch straight through?
3. **Staging**: Does the eye immediately know where to go without explanation?
4. **Straight Ahead vs Pose to Pose**: Which production approach serves this beat?
5. **Follow Through & Overlap**: Have we earned the settle and trailing details?
6. **Slow In & Slow Out**: Are easing curves consistent across every shot?
7. **Arc**: Does the world move organically in curves, breaking only intentionally?
8. **Secondary Action**: What small supporting moments bring the world alive?
9. **Timing**: Is the tempo locked early before expensive downstream renders?
10. **Exaggeration**: Is stylization calibrated to the exact genre tone?
11. **Solid Drawing**: Do all shots feel like one spatially coherent world?
12. **Appeal**: Do people want to keep watching?

## Review Checklist
- Matches the established motion language
- Serves the story/beat need
- Craft level consistent across all shots
- Early tests validated before full production
