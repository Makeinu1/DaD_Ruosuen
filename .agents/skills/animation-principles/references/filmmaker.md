# Filmmaker: Cinematic Animation Craft

> Source: [dylantarre/animation-principles — skills/03-by-role-persona/filmmaker/SKILL.md](https://github.com/dylantarre/animation-principles/blob/main/skills/03-by-role-persona/filmmaker/SKILL.md) (MIT License)

Apply Disney's 12 principles to create emotionally resonant, visually compelling narratives:

1. **Squash and Stretch** — Emotional elasticity. Characters physically embody emotional states; objects reflect story weight.
2. **Anticipation** — Build tension and setup payoffs. Longer anticipation sets up a bigger release; quick cuts subvert for surprise.
3. **Staging** — Visual storytelling through composition. What is in frame, what is lit, what moves, and foreground/background relationships carry the story.
4. **Straight Ahead vs Pose to Pose** — Straight ahead for spontaneity and organic chaos (water, snow, wind); pose to pose for choreographed climax beats.
5. **Follow Through and Overlapping Action** — Physical continuity (costume, fins, wake, petals) and emotional follow-through (reactions trail events).
6. **Slow In and Slow Out** — Pacing within shots. Slow ease-in builds anticipation; slow ease-out extends emotional beats.
7. **Arc** — Natural movement paths. Crane shots, dolly arcs, creature glides, and character emotional arcs mirror physical arcs.
8. **Secondary Action** — Environmental storytelling supporting the primary beat (lantern flame flicker, ripples, trembling buds).
9. **Timing** — Rhythm and pacing. Fast cutting for tension, long takes for intimacy, dramatic pauses before revelation.
10. **Exaggeration** — Tonal control (~10% subtle push for poetic realism; bold push for stylization). Match genre.
11. **Solid Drawing** — Spatial coherence across cuts: 180-degree rule, consistent eyelines, screen direction, legible geography.
12. **Appeal** — Worlds and silhouettes audiences want to spend time in; visual beauty serving story.

## Cinematic Checklist
- Every frame advances story or character
- Motion motivated by emotion or intention
- Technical craft invisible to audience
- Serves the story, not the ego
